# PIDKI

Pidki is a collaborative savings app that brings the traditional Latin American tanda system into the digital age. It allows groups of friends, family, or coworkers to pool money together in regular rounds — whether weekly or monthly — and take turns receiving the full pot. Each participant contributes a fixed amount every round, and through a fair random draw, one member is selected to collect the total sum. It's a simple, trust-based way to help everyone in the group reach their financial goals, make a big purchase, or build savings — without banks, interest rates, or complicated paperwork.

Rules:

1. A user can create a circle from 3 persons up to 10 maximum.
2. The users that creates the circle is the first to receive the payment for the first round.
